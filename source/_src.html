Team

  • Robert Satcher Sr.

    Founder & Partner

    Robert Satcher Sr. is the Founder of Tealwater Capital. He brings more than thirty years of experience in sales, management, and corporate finance to the firm.

    In 1998, Robert co-founded Softdocs, a Columbia, South Carolina software company building document management, electronic forms, and workflow automation for higher education and K‑12 institutions. He served as Chief Financial Officer and Executive Vice President through the company’s growth, and Softdocs was acquired by Ridgemont Equity Partners in January 2021.

    At Tealwater Capital, Robert takes the long view. He oversees the firm’s decision‑making, evaluates investment opportunities, and guides how capital is allocated across the portfolio.

    Robert holds a B.S. in Business Management and a master’s in Business from Clemson University, where he serves on the Board of Visitors. He has also served on the Ben Lippen School Board and as president of its booster club, and has served multiple terms as a deacon at his church. He is based in Greenville, South Carolina.
